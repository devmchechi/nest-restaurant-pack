# Menu Margin Optimizer

Classifies restaurant dishes into Stars, Plowhorses, Puzzles, and Dogs based on POS sales volume and recipe food costs, generating data-backed pricing and menu engineering strategies.

## Working Folder Structure

- POS Sales Data: `./pos/`
- Recipe Cost Cards: `./recipes/`
- Audit Reports: `./reports/`

## Example Prompts

- "Run a menu engineering audit on the dinner sales in ./pos/dinner_october.csv."
- "Which popular dishes have high food costs and need price adjustments?"
- "Identify the Dogs on our menu that should be replaced with higher-margin items."
