# Restaurant Operations Pack

Operational intelligence designed for independent restaurants, cafes, bars, and catering businesses. Optimizes recipe food costs through menu engineering, tracks kitchen inventory waste to recommend optimal par levels, and crafts personalized responses to online guest reviews across Google, Yelp, and OpenTable.

## Included Agents

- **Menu Margin Optimizer (`menu-margin-optimizer`):** Performs recipe costing and sales volume analysis to classify dishes into Stars, Plowhorses, Puzzles, and Dogs.
- **Inventory Waste Tracker (`inventory-waste-tracker`):** Analyzes food prep sheets and spoilage logs to project accurate order par levels and reduce food waste.
- **Guest Review Responder (`guest-review-responder`):** Monitors diner sentiment across platforms and drafts thoughtful, de-escalating review responses.

## Included Workflow

- **Daily Restaurant Ops Review (`daily-restaurant-ops-review`):** Evaluates guest review sentiment, audits kitchen inventory waste, and recommends profitable daily menu specials.
